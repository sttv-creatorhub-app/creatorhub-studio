**Fixes the speech-recognition download.** Captions and Clip Generator need FFmpeg with speech recognition. The copy the app downloaded didn't include it, so the "download" prompt kept coming back. The app now downloads a build that has it (about 270 MB, once).

**Clip Generator**
- **Volume spikes.** A sudden jump from quiet to loud now counts as a clippable moment, measured against how loud that video usually gets. Clips start about 5 seconds before the volume takes off and end once things calm down.
- **Pauses after talking.** A silence right after a conversation (the end of a story, a line landing) is picked up too, as a lighter signal. Those clips cover the talk leading up to it and end on the pause.
- The clips pre-selected for you are now the 5 best by score.
