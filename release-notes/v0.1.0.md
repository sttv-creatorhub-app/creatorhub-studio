**First public release of Clip Studio.** 🎬

**What you can do**
- Import MP4, MOV and MKV videos, or drag them onto the preview.
- Record yourself over a folder of B-roll, or over your screen.
- Choose **Fill** to crop to the frame, or **Fit** to show the whole picture over a blurred copy.
- Trim takes and add text, pictures, zooms and transitions.
- Export 1080p MP4 in 9:16 or 16:9, with loudness levelled for phones.

**Pro**
- Automatic captions, made on your PC.
- No watermark on exports.

**Install**
Download `Creator-Hub-Studio-Setup-0.1.0.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
