**New: Stream Backdrop (free).** An animated background for your stream that you add to OBS as a Browser Source. Pick a scene and colours, let it react to your mic, or have it follow the colours on your screen.

**Put your own things on it**
- **Text boxes**, for socials, schedules or anything else.
- **Counters**, like "Deaths: 12" or "5 wins".
- **Timers:** a stopwatch ("Live for 1:02:05"), a countdown (for a length, or until a time) or a clock.
- **Images**, like your logo.
- **Placing them:** drag them into place on the preview, and they show up in exactly the same spot in OBS. Choose the font, size, colour, outline and shadow, and add a box behind the text.
- **Hotkeys** for counters (+1, −1, reset) and timers (start/pause, reset). They work while your game has focus.
- Counter values and running timers are kept when you close the app.

**Setup:** in OBS, add a Browser Source with the address shown on the Stream Backdrop page (usually `http://127.0.0.1:7420/backdrop`), 1920×1080.
