**New: Twitch Clips.** Grab clips straight from your Twitch channel and turn them into TikToks and Shorts.

**Twitch Clips**
- **Connect Twitch** with a short code you approve on twitch.tv. Your password never goes through the app, and the login is stored encrypted on your PC.
- **Browse your clips** with thumbnails, views, dates and who clipped them. Filter by time range and search by title.
- **Clips from channels you edit for:** add the channel by name.
- **Download** clips to `Videos\Creator Hub Twitch Clips`, or **send them to Clip Studio**, where each clip becomes its own video.
- **Facecam split for a whole batch:** turn on Include facecam when sending, mark your facecam and gameplay once, and the same layout is used for every clip.

Twitch Clips is free. Downloads use Twitch's official clip download, for your own channel and channels you're an editor of.

**Fixes**
- Clips imported at the same moment no longer clash with each other.
