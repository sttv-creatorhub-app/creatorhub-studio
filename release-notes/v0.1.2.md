**New: a simpler way to start a video, and facecam split screens for imported clips.**

**Starting a video**
- **New video** now asks how you want to make it: **B-roll + my face** or **Import a video**. Your camera and mic stay off until you choose.

**B-roll + my face**
- Choose **Cut-out** (your background removed) or **Split screen** before you start.
- The B-roll you pick now **plays on a loop from your start point** while you get ready, and it jumps there as you move the start slider.

**Import a video**
- Turn on **Include facecam** to make a split screen from a stream clip.
- Drag a **Facecam** box over your camera and a **Gameplay** box over the game. A live preview shows the result.
- Choose camera on top or bottom (left or right in 16:9), and how much space the gameplay gets.
- Projects now only show the controls that apply to them: no Record button in import projects, and no import button in B-roll projects.

**Fixes**
- Phone videos recorded sideways now import the right way round.
- Zooms on imported videos aim at the right spot.
- The trim timeline and caption list are styled properly again.

**Removed**
- Recording your screen as the background. Older projects that used it still open and export.
