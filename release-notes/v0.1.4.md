**New: Clip Generator (Pro).** Drop in a stream VOD, even one that's hours long, and it finds the moments worth clipping.

**Clip Generator**
- **Built for long VODs.** A quick pass over the whole video's sound, then a close listen around the likely moments. A 3-hour stream takes a few minutes on a PC with a recent graphics card.
- **Fast or Thorough.** Fast listens around the likely moments; Thorough listens to every word.
- **Stop any time.** Finished parts are saved, so a scan picks up where it left off, even after closing the app.
- **Finds** big reactions, sustained hype, and what you said ("let's go", "no way", "clip that"…), with more clips for longer videos.
- **Twitch viewer clips.** Match the video to one of your past Twitch broadcasts, and moments your viewers clipped are found first.
- **Send to Clip Studio.** Pick the moments you like; each one is cut out and opens in Clip Studio, with the facecam layout marked once for the whole batch.

**Fixes**
- Automatic captions no longer show sound labels like "BLANK_AUDIO" or "MUSIC PLAYING", and fewer words get split in two ("Care ful").
- FFmpeg can be downloaded from the Clip Generator page.
