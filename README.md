# Clip Studio by Creator Hub

Turn the videos you already have into TikToks, Shorts and Reels. A free Windows app.

- **Import a video** you already have, or record yourself over B-roll.
- **Pull clips from Twitch**: connect your channel and send clips straight into Clip Studio.
- **Trim it**, add text, pictures, zooms and transitions.
- **Export a 1080p MP4** in 9:16 for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, or 16:9 for YouTube.
- **Everything runs on your PC.** Nothing gets uploaded.

## Download

Get the latest installer from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)**: `Creator-Hub-Studio-Setup-<version>.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11. On first launch the app downloads FFmpeg (about 160 MB), the free video tool it uses. Installed copies update themselves.

> **Windows says it "protected your PC"?** New apps from small developers show that warning until they build up a reputation. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
